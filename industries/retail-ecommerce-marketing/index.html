Plan the Collection, Campaign and Customer Journey

Creative and promotion need to reflect the products that are available and the action your business can support.

Prepare the Product Story

Agree the collection, customer group and the product information that matters: images, variants, availability, pricing and the approved offer. Use photography and short video to show the product clearly.

Match Promotion to the Buying Route

Choose the relevant social, paid-media, website or WhatsApp route. Confirm whether customers should visit a store, ask a question or complete an online checkout, and make that next step clear.

Review Enquiries and Sales Separately

Use available campaign, website and sales information to assess the agreed objective. Check where customers stop or need help; views, clicks and messages should not be presented as completed purchases.

Define the Scope Before a Launch

The useful plan depends on the range, locations, buying route and capacity of the team handling customers.

What Your Team Provides

Bring the product range, priority collections, target regions, approved prices and offers, stock information and brand assets. Agree who approves creative, answers enquiries and confirms product or delivery details.

What to Agree in the Proposal

Specify the content and production required, the promotion channels, website work, reporting and follow-up responsibilities. Advertising, production and third-party platform costs should be identified where applicable; no fixed sales return is assumed.
