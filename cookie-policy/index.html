2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small data files that may be stored on your browser or device when you visit a website. Similar technologies, including pixels, tags and local storage, may also be used to recognise devices, measure website usage and support advertising or analytics functions.

3. How Totem Uses Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies for purposes including:

  • Operating and securing the website
  • Understanding website traffic and visitor behaviour
  • Measuring page views and website interactions
  • Understanding the effectiveness of marketing campaigns
  • Supporting advertising measurement and attribution
  • Improving website performance and user experience

4. Essential and Functional Technologies

Some technologies may be necessary for the proper operation, security or functionality of the website.

These technologies may support functions such as form submission, security controls, navigation, session handling and technical website operation.

5. Analytics Technologies

Our website uses analytics technologies to help us understand how visitors use the website.

At present, this includes Google Analytics. Google Analytics may collect information such as pages visited, approximate location derived from network information, browser and device characteristics, referral source and interaction data.

We use analytics information to understand website performance, identify useful content and improve the user experience.

6. Advertising and Measurement Technologies

Our website also uses advertising-related technologies, including Meta Pixel, for purposes such as advertising measurement, campaign attribution and understanding interactions with our website.

These technologies may allow the relevant provider to recognise a browser or device and associate website activity with information held by that provider, subject to the provider’s own privacy terms and account settings.

7. Third-Party Technologies

Some cookies, pixels or similar technologies used on this website are provided by third parties.

These may include services from:

  • Google
  • Meta
  • WhatsApp
  • Website hosting and infrastructure providers
  • Other technology providers used to operate or measure the website

Third-party providers may process information according to their own privacy policies, cookie policies and service terms.

8. Information That May Be Collected

Depending on the technology used, information collected automatically may include:

  • IP address
  • Browser type
  • Device type
  • Operating system
  • Pages visited
  • Time and duration of visits
  • Referring website or campaign
  • Website interactions
  • Advertising and analytics identifiers

9. Managing Cookies

You can usually control or delete cookies through your browser settings.

Most browsers allow you to:

  • View stored cookies
  • Delete existing cookies
  • Block some or all cookies
  • Receive warnings before cookies are stored

Blocking certain cookies or technologies may affect website functionality or the accuracy of analytics and advertising measurement.

10. Advertising Preferences

You may also be able to manage advertising preferences through the privacy and advertising controls provided by platforms such as Google and Meta.

These controls are managed by the relevant third-party provider and may vary according to your account, device and region.

11. Cookie Duration

Some cookies may exist only for the duration of a browsing session, while others may remain on a device for a longer period.

The duration of third-party cookies is determined by the relevant service provider and may change from time to time.

12. Consent and Regional Requirements

Cookie and tracking requirements may vary depending on the visitor’s location and applicable law.

Where applicable, additional consent or preference controls may be required before certain analytics or advertising technologies are activated.
