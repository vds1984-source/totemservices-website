Why Totem Exists

Our Vision

Build Totem into one of India's leading integrated marketing and business-growth companies, with the long-term ambition of being recognised among the country's leading agencies.

Our Values

Strategy before activity • Measurable growth • Accountability • Creativity • Continuous learning • Transparency • Client partnership

Our Approach

One Connected System Instead of Disconnected Marketing Activity.

Businesses often need strategy, content, design, advertising, search, websites and follow-up working together. Totem's approach is to connect these disciplines around the commercial objective rather than treat each one as an isolated activity.

Strategy First

We begin with the business objective, audience, market, positioning and customer journey before deciding which marketing activities should be prioritised.

Integrated Execution

Content, creative production, social media, advertising, SEO, websites and technology can work together within one coordinated marketing system.

Measurement & Accountability

Marketing should be connected to meaningful signals such as visibility, enquiries, leads, conversion paths and business outcomes wherever measurement is available.
