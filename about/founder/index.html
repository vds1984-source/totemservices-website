Why Totem Was Built

Strategy Before Activity.

Totem's approach is based on the belief that marketing should begin with business direction, customer understanding and a clear commercial objective before choosing channels, campaigns or creative execution.

The Integrated Approach

Businesses often need strategy, content, design, advertising, search visibility, websites and follow-up working together rather than being handled as disconnected activities.

Totem is structured around bringing these disciplines together through one coordinated marketing and growth system.

The Opportunity

High-Quality Marketing Should Be Accessible Beyond Major Cities.

One of the ideas behind Totem is that ambitious businesses should be able to access structured strategy, professional creative execution and modern marketing technology irrespective of city size.

Direction

Marketing activity should be connected to a clear business objective rather than performed only because a channel or format is popular.

Integration

Strategy, content, performance marketing, search, websites and follow-up work better when they support one connected customer journey.

Accountability

A coordinated team creates clearer ownership across planning, execution, measurement and continuous improvement.
