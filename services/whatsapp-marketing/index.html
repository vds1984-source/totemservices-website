Plan the Conversation and the Response

WhatsApp marketing works as part of a customer journey. Message volume alone does not show whether a contact received useful help or became a customer.

1. Define the Audience and Permission

Review how contacts were collected, the purpose of the message and the available permission records. Agree how preferences and requests to stop messages will be handled.

2. Build Relevant Message Paths

Match messages to the agreed enquiry or customer stage. Confirm the offer, content, timing, approval process and the person who will answer when a customer replies.

3. Review Replies and Follow-Up

Track available message and response signals separately from qualified enquiries and sales. Use the results and customer feedback to refine the next sequence.

Start With Your Contact and Reply Process

A useful brief covers both the outgoing message and what your team can do when someone responds.

Information to Bring

Explain the customer group, where the contacts came from, the purpose of the sequence and who manages conversations. Share approved offers, opening hours and the next action customers should be able to take.

Confirm the Delivery Scope

Agree the WhatsApp account, supported tools, template or platform requirements, integrations and any provider fees before implementation. Automation and delivery depend on the chosen platform and account setup; a message or reply does not confirm a sale.

Use CRM workflow planning to organise responses, and social media content to support the wider customer conversation. Discuss the proposed activity before sharing a contact list.
