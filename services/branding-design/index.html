How we work

Discover → Define → Design → Apply → Refine

01 Brand Discovery

Understand the business, audience, positioning, competitors, existing assets and the practical applications the design must support.

02 Visual Direction

Define the visual character, typography, colour direction and design language before producing the wider set of brand assets.

03 Identity & Creative Design

Create the required logo, identity elements, social creatives, campaign designs, brochures, packaging or other agreed materials.

04 Real-World Application

Adapt the design system across digital, print and physical touchpoints so customers receive a consistent visual experience.

05 Review & Approval

Organise feedback and revisions around the approved direction rather than redesigning each asset independently.

06 Ongoing Consistency

Use the established visual direction as the reference for future campaign creatives, social content, print materials and signage.
