Website Design & Development for Kurukshetra Businesses

A business website should do more than present information. It should explain your offer clearly, build trust, support search visibility and make it easy for potential customers to call, message, enquire or take the next step.

Business Websites

Responsive websites structured around your services, audience, proof, location and conversion goals so visitors quickly understand what you offer and why they should contact you.

Landing Pages & Funnels

Focused pages for advertising and lead generation campaigns with clear messaging, strong calls to action, forms and WhatsApp journeys designed to reduce friction.

Tracking & Integrations

GA4, Meta Pixel, CRM-ready forms and WhatsApp integration help connect website activity with enquiry tracking, follow-up and future optimisation.

How we work

Plan → Structure → Design → Build → Measure

01 Business & Audience Discovery

Clarify the offer, target audience, priority services, proof points, locations and the actions visitors should take.

02 Information Architecture

Plan page hierarchy, navigation and content flow so users and search engines can understand the website clearly.

03 UI/UX & Content Direction

Design responsive layouts around readability, trust, brand consistency and conversion rather than visual decoration alone.

04 Development & Integration

Build the responsive website and connect required lead forms, WhatsApp, analytics, pixels and CRM-ready workflows.

05 SEO & Technical Review

Check titles, metadata, internal links, canonical signals, structured data, performance and crawlability before launch.

06 Measurement & Improvement

Use website and enquiry data to identify where pages, messaging or conversion paths can be improved over time.
