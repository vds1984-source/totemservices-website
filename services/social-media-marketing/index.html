Frequently asked questions

Social Media Marketing — FAQs

What can be included in social media management?

The agreed scope can include strategy, content planning, scripts, photography, Reels, editing, design, captions, client approvals, publishing and monthly performance reporting.

Are social media posting and paid ads the same service?

Organic content builds communication and audience engagement. Meta and Google advertising involve separate campaign planning and media spend; we agree the content, campaign scope and budget with you before starting.

How do you plan the next month’s content?

We combine the monthly performance review with current trends, customer pain points and client feedback to agree the next month’s objectives and content roadmap.
