Technical SEO

Frequently asked questions

SEO, Local SEO & GEO — FAQs

What SEO services does Totem provide?

We cover technical SEO, on-page SEO, content SEO, internal linking, local SEO, Google Business Profile optimisation, citations, schema, Search Console measurement and GEO or AI-search visibility.

Can SEO help a local Kurukshetra business?

Yes. Local SEO can improve visibility for relevant location-based searches by strengthening your website, Google Business Profile, local pages, citations, reviews and business information consistency.

What is GEO?

GEO, or generative engine optimisation, focuses on making your business and expertise easier for AI-driven search and answer systems to understand through clear content, structured data and strong entity signals.
