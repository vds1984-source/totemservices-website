SEO & Local SEO: Common Questions

What does a local SEO engagement include?

The agreed scope can include a website and Google Business Profile audit, keyword and page planning, technical fixes, content improvements, citations, review guidance and reporting. We prioritise the work around your business goals and existing assets.

Can Totem guarantee a number-one ranking?

No. Search and Maps results vary by query, location, competition and other factors. We agree a practical scope and measure relevant visibility and enquiry signals rather than promise a fixed ranking.

How is SEO different from Google Ads?

SEO improves organic discoverability through your website and business information. Google Ads buys advertising placements using a separate media budget. They can support the same enquiry journey, but their costs and results should be reported separately.

How much do SEO services cost?

The proposal depends on your website, competition, content requirements, locations and implementation needs. We define the deliverables and reporting before quoting. Advertising spend, production and third-party tools are identified separately where applicable.

What will the monthly report show?

The report separates Search Console impressions and website clicks, Business Profile actions and tracked enquiries. Where lead records are available, we review qualified enquiries and follow-up outcomes with you. Clicks and profile interactions are not confirmed customers.

What is GEO or AI-search optimisation?

GEO describes work to make useful business content easier to discover and understand in AI search. For Google’s AI features, established SEO practices apply; no special AI file or schema is required, and visibility is not guaranteed.
