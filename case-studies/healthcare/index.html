How Totem evaluates healthcare growth

We don't stop at reach or leads. We measure the path to business.

Visibility

Views, reach, search impressions and local discovery.

Intent

Profile visits, website visits, calls, directions and WhatsApp actions.

Lead

Form fills, messages and campaign-attributed enquiries where tracking exists.

Qualification

Serviceability, geography and genuine treatment/service interest.

Appointment

Tracked only where the client provides reliable appointment-stage data.

Business outcome

Revenue/patient outcome is never inferred from an upstream platform metric.
