How the content strategy evolved

Three months, three increasingly useful creative signals

JUNE

Doctor expertise and patient journeys created attention

June reported 134,410 views and 90,828 accounts reached. The strongest pieces included a Dr Simran Virk doctor-talk reel at 63K views, a patient-journey reel at 56K and an infertility-awareness reel at 48K. The month also recorded four Instagram leads on ₹3,000 reported spend.

JULY

Problem-first fertility education became the repeatable hook

July rose to 261,841 reported views and 2,469 profile visits. Uterine-fibroid awareness reached 63K views and a hormonal-imbalance/fertility-anatomy explainer reached 56K. The reporting identified a clear pattern: relatable health problems followed by useful medical explanation performed strongly.

AUGUST

Emotionally honest patient-centred storytelling became the strongest signal

August recorded 249,411 views and 171,705 unique viewers. A miscarriage-awareness reel led at 75K views and +6 new followers, followed by a doctor-consultation reel at 56K and newborn/baby content at 51K.

Discovery & local relevance

Virk repeatedly reached people beyond its follower base

Discovery

Non-follower exposure remained extremely high across the reports: 96.2% in June and 98.1% in July and August. This is evidence of discovery beyond the existing audience—not evidence of appointments.

Regional audience signal

July audience-location reporting identified Thanesar at 10.4%, Pehowa at 5.7%, Karnal at 3.7% and Babain at 3.4%, giving the campaign a measurable local/regional context around Kurukshetra.

The most important finding

Visibility alone was not enough

What worked

Reels repeatedly outperformed static formats, while doctor-led education, problem-first health hooks and sensitive patient-centred storytelling emerged as the strongest creative directions.

What needed improvement

July produced 2,469 profile visits but zero recorded bio-link taps and zero address taps. The next optimisation problem was therefore not simply “more reach”; it was creating a private, low-friction path from interest to enquiry.

Totem principle: Visibility → Intent → Enquiry → Qualified Lead → Appointment → Business outcome. A campaign should be measured according to the stage the available data can actually verify.

What this case demonstrates

A fertility marketing system needs content and conversion architecture

Doctor-led authority

Clinical expertise translated into short-form educational content people could understand.

Problem-first education

Health concerns such as fibroids and hormonal imbalance created relevant entry points into fertility education.

Patient-centred storytelling

Emotionally honest fertility experiences produced the strongest August content signal.

Selective paid amplification

June and July reports documented ₹3,000 monthly Meta promotion, while results were kept separate from organic and profile metrics.

Local audience context

Audience-location data helped evaluate whether discovery was relevant to the clinic's regional market.

Conversion diagnosis

Profile traffic without link movement identified where the next optimisation work was needed.
