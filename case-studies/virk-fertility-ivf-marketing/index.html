IVF & Fertility Marketing Case Study · Kurukshetra

From Clinical Education to Patient-Centred Fertility Storytelling

From June to August 2026, Totem produced doctor-led fertility education and patient-centred content for Virk Fertility in Kurukshetra. This case study describes the work, the role of paid promotion and the Instagram activity recorded in the reports.

How the content strategy evolved

How the creative focus changed

JUNE

Doctor-led education and patient stories

The June report records 134,410 views and 90,828 accounts reached. Most of its reported views were attributed to ads. The report highlights a Dr Simran Virk doctor-talk reel, a patient-journey reel and an infertility-awareness reel. It also records four Instagram leads and ₹3,000 in promotion spend.

JULY

Fertility education built around common concerns

The July report records 261,841 views and 2,469 profile visits. The content covered uterine fibroids, hormonal imbalance and fertility anatomy, using familiar concerns to introduce medical explanations.

AUGUST

Patient-centred storytelling

The August report records 249,411 views and 171,705 unique viewers for its 30-day window. It lists a miscarriage-awareness reel at 75K views, a doctor-consultation reel at 56K and newborn content at 51K.

Discovery & local relevance

Virk repeatedly reached people beyond its follower base

Discovery

Non-follower exposure remained extremely high across the reports: 96.2% in June and 98.1% in July and August. The percentages describe audience discovery. Appointment counts require separate records.

Regional audience signal

July audience-location reporting identified Thanesar at 10.4%, Pehowa at 5.7%, Karnal at 3.7% and Babain at 3.4%, giving the campaign a measurable local/regional context around Kurukshetra.

Reading the enquiry signals

Profile visits and the contact route

Creative themes in the reports

The reports highlight doctor-led education, explainers on common fertility concerns and patient-centred stories. They use different format breakdowns, which limits comparisons between reels and static posts.

What the July contact metrics showed

July recorded 2,469 profile visits, zero bio-link taps and zero address taps. These figures give the team a reason to review the contact route. Calls, direct messages and enquiries require separate measurement.

Totem principle: Visibility → Intent → Enquiry → Qualified Lead → Appointment → Business outcome. A campaign should be measured according to the stage the available data can actually verify.

What this case demonstrates

A fertility marketing system needs content and conversion architecture

Doctor-led authority

Clinical expertise translated into short-form educational content people could understand.

Problem-first education

Health concerns such as fibroids and hormonal imbalance created relevant entry points into fertility education.

Patient-centred storytelling

The August report lists miscarriage awareness among the most viewed topics.

Selective paid amplification

June and July each reported ₹3,000 in paid promotion. The reports provide different levels of detail on paid distribution, so the combined view figure includes paid and other activity without a comparable breakdown across the three windows.

Local audience context

Audience-location data helped evaluate whether discovery was relevant to the clinic's regional market.

Contact-route review

Profile visits and link taps can help guide a review of the contact route. Assess calls, direct messages and enquiry records alongside them.

Evidence & methodology

How to read the numbers

Data sources

This case study is based on Totem's Virk Fertility Instagram performance reports for June, July and August 2026. Metrics are reported according to the platform/reporting windows shown in those source reports.

Important limitations

The four reported Instagram leads apply to June 2026. July and August figures describe platform activity. A three-month count of patients or appointments would require separate verified records.
