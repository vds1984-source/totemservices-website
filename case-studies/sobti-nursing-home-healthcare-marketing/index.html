The strategy

A connected healthcare marketing system

Trust-building content

Doctor-led educational videos, patient-focused stories, technology showcases and procedure-related content made clinical expertise visible and understandable.

Reels-led discovery

Short-form video became the principal reach format. Reels accounted for 91.8% of Instagram views in June and 96% of Facebook views in August.

High-intent Google Search

Search campaigns captured active eye-care demand through keyword refinement, ad-copy optimisation, call actions and appointment-focused sitelinks.

Local search visibility

Google Business Profile was treated as a conversion asset, with calls and driving directions monitored as high-intent local actions.

Meta lead generation

Paid social supported enquiry generation while geographic quality and serviceability of leads were reviewed alongside volume.

Measurement infrastructure

GA4 and Google Tag Manager were integrated with phone-click, WhatsApp-click and landing-page behaviour included in measurement.

June → July → August 2026

What the data revealed

JUNE

Reach accelerated

Instagram produced 1,126,482 views and reached 782,164 accounts. Profile activity reached 14,562, up 153.1% versus May. Eye-surgery and doctor-led Reels were among the strongest content.

JULY

Social scale + measurable search acquisition

Instagram reached 1,274,475 views. Google Search generated 5,741 impressions, 546 clicks and 306 tracked conversions on ₹6,080.72 spend, with a reported ₹19.87 cost per tracked conversion.

AUGUST

Strong visibility, but lead quality required intervention

Instagram and Facebook still generated more than 1.84 million combined views and Google Business Profile produced 721 interactions. Meta leads, however, fell from 248 in July to 165 in August, prompting a deeper diagnosis.

Transparent optimisation

When performance changed, Totem diagnosed the system

What the audit found

A temporary Facebook recommendation restriction affected surgery-related content distribution. Meta location settings also allowed enquiries beyond the intended local catchment. Offer-led creative attracted lower-intent enquiries, while a high-performing Instagram Reel had been removed during the month.

Why lead quality mattered

August produced 165 Meta leads versus 248 in July, but enquiries appeared from Delhi, Uttar Pradesh, Hisar, Rewari and Patiala—outside the intended local catchment. The optimisation objective therefore shifted toward serviceable lead quality rather than simply maximising form fills.

Measurement principle: reach, clicks, calls, direction requests, leads and tracked conversions represent different funnel stages. This case study does not combine them and call the total “patients”.
Corrective action

Turning reporting into action

Geo-targeting correction

Tighten Meta delivery to residents in the intended catchment and monitor lead geography.

Lead qualification

Refine offer creative and add qualification so stronger enquiries can be prioritised.

Faster response process

Reduce lead-response delay with immediate notifications and same-day follow-up.

Search refinement

Strengthen high-intent exact/phrase keyword groups and use search-term reviews and negative keywords to reduce irrelevant paid traffic.

Local trust reinforcement

Connect Google Business Profile more closely with search advertising while maintaining reviews, posts, photos and local visibility.

Cleaner attribution

Improve conversion definitions and call-source tracking across paid search, organic and local-profile activity.

Key learnings

What this healthcare marketing engagement demonstrates

Educational authority creates discovery

Doctor-led educational content repeatedly ranked among the stronger formats.

Patient stories create trust

A patient testimonial was the strongest reported Facebook post in August at 116,160 views.

Local intent needs its own strategy

Hundreds of calls and direction requests showed how close Google Business Profile can sit to real-world action.

Reach is not conversion

Large social reach did not prevent a decline in Meta lead quality; the full enquiry journey had to be measured.

Tracking improves decisions

GA4/GTM and action tracking created a stronger basis for optimisation than impressions and views alone.

Transparency builds trust

The August decline was documented with identifiable causes and corrective actions rather than hidden behind vanity metrics.
